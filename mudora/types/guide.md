# Replication guides

**Marker:** `<meta name="mudora-type" content="guide">`
**Starter:** none yet; reuse `starters/base.css`

A guide tells someone how to do something themselves, end to end.

## Rules

- **Steps as a path** (rule 15): what the reader does, in order; versions, commands and sources in hover tips or small text.
- **Step labels name the place** as a bare noun (rule 21).
- **One path per device kind** when the steps differ (rule 23 names).
- Prerequisites the reader must already have go first, in one line each.

## Checklist

- Could a newcomer follow the steps without asking anyone?
- Does every step label name where it happens?
- Is every command reachable (hover or copy) but out of the main text?
